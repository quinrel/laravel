# Holvek Developer Documentation

Welcome to the official developer documentation for **Holvek**, engineered by **Skoorite Limited**.

**Holvek** is a Laravel-native visibility and discovery intelligence toolkit that helps businesses understand their visibility across discovery surfaces (search engines, social media, answer engines, maps, directories, etc.) and helps them take action.

The free standalone tier optimizes your site for both search engines (SEO) and generative engines (GEO). It is optimized to compete excellently with paid versions of your favorite SEO tools.

The Holvek Cloud-connected tier gives you monumental intelligence and hands you quick fixes to improve your brand discoverability wherever your audience is.

Holvek operates via a highly optimized React SPA interface and communicates exclusively through secure API endpoints within your Laravel application.

## Who is this for?
This documentation portal is designed for:
* **Laravel Developers:** Engineers integrating the package into their host applications, customizing configuration, and extending Eloquent models with visibility metadata.
* **Server Administrators:** Ops teams managing Laravel applications at scale who need to monitor background queues, Horizon workers, and troubleshooting runbooks.
* **Agency Partners:** Teams requiring a deep technical understanding of brand discoverability (`llms.txt`) and advanced Schema.org implementations.
