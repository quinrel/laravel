# Holvek Developer Documentation

Welcome to the official developer documentation for **Holvek**, engineered by **Skoorite Limited**.

**Holvek** for laravel is a laravel-native visibility and discovery intelligence toolkit that help businesses understand their visibility across discovery surfaces (search engines, social media, answer engines, maps, directories, etc) and help them act. 

The free standalone mode does not only optimise your site for search engines (SEO), generative engines (GEO), its optimized to compete excellently with paid versions of your favourite SEO tool.

The holvek cloud connected tier give you monumental intelligence and hands you quick fix to improve your brand discoverability everywhere your audience are.

Holvek operates via a highly optimized React interface and communicates exclusively through secure REST endpoints.


## Who is this for?
This documentation portal is designed for:
* **Headless Developers:** Engineers building decoupled frontends (Next.js, React, Vue) who need to audit and consume discovery intelligence via our REST API.
* **Server Administrators:** Ops teams managing WordPress at scale who need to monitor background queues, cron limits, and troubleshooting runbooks.
* **Agency Partners:** Teams requiring deep technical understanding of their brand discoverability (`llms.txt`) and advanced Schema.org implementations.
