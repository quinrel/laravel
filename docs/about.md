# About Holvek

Holvek is an all-in-one search engine optimization (SEO) package for Laravel. It helps website owners, content creators, and businesses make their content easy to find on search engines like Google and Bing, as well as modern AI-powered search tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

---

## Why We Built Holvek
The current change in search landscape has also made traditional SEO tools grossly inadequate. 
Businesses want to focus on there core operations and drive growth rather than juggling between multiple tools that help them reach out to customers. 
Majority of the seo tools are bloated and require some level of expertise of get meaning results with them.

Hence, we built Holvek to help businesses reach their clients, customers whereever they are. Holvek strives to bring all your seo and discoverability needs into a single dashboard that doesnt just help you understand and monitor your discoverability, it hands you ready to fixes to improve discovery. 

Search has also changed. Today, people discover websites not only through blue links on Google, but through AI overviews, chatbots, social media answer engines, and mobile assistants.

Holvek was built to solve these problems with three simple ideas:

1. **Keep it lightweight:** An SEO package should never slow down your website. We keep our code clean, run heavy tasks in the background, and add minimal overhead to your pages.
2. **Make SEO easy to understand:** We explain recommendations in plain English without confusing acronyms or buzzwords.
3. **Be ready for both search engines and AI:** We help you structure your content so that both search engine bots and AI tools can read, understand, and cite your work accurately.

## How It Works

### Smart Defaults Right Out of the Box
The moment you install Holvek, it gets to work. The package automatically creates XML sitemaps, sets up standard Open Graph tags for social sharing, generates breadcrumbs, and adds valid Schema.org structured data. You do not need to configure dozens of complicated settings just to get started.

### Helpful On-Page Writing Guidance
When writing in the application, Holvek gives you practical, actionable advice:
- Checks whether your title and description fit comfortably on mobile and desktop screens.
- Evaluates text readability using the established Flesch-Kincaid scale.
- Shows live previews of how your post will look on Google, Facebook, and Twitter/X.
- Suggests links to other relevant articles on your site to keep readers engaged.

### Search Engine Notifications
Holvek can use IndexNow to notify participating search engines about published URLs when an administrator enables it. Google’s Indexing API is restricted to eligible JobPosting and livestream pages; neither notification guarantees indexing.

### Built-in Technical Tools
You don't need five different packages to manage basic website health. Holvek includes:
- A redirect manager for 301 and 302 redirects.
- A 404 error monitor to catch and fix broken URLs.
- A visual robots.txt editor with syntax validation.
- A broken link checker that finds dead hyperlinks in your content.

### Clear Performance & Search Insights
Connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 to see real traffic numbers, search clicks, and top-ranking keywords directly inside your application dashboard.

### Optional AI Assistance
If you want help writing titles, meta descriptions, or summaries, you can connect your preferred AI provider (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Ollama) or use the Holvek remote service. Ollama defaults to this server. A remote Ollama host receives the text you submit. AI features are completely optional—the package works fully without any third-party AI keys.

## Our Core Guarantees

- **No Bloat:** We don't display intrusive banner ads, upsell popups, or unnecessary widgets in your application admin.
- **Zero Lock-In:** Your data belongs to you. You can easily import data from legacy SEO packages, or export your settings at any time.
- **Privacy-First:** We respect visitor privacy. Holvek includes a lightweight cookie notice that records a visitor’s choice; it does not replace a full consent-management solution for third-party scripts.
- **Open Standards:** Everything we generate adheres to official web standards, including Schema.org JSON-LD specifications, Google Search documentation, and emerging AI standards like `llms.txt`.
